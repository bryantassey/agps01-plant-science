## Run and play

Open index.html in a browser for local touch play. Use the HTTPS GitHub Pages address for phone motion and wake lock. Set up two to four players with first names and last initials. Give clues without saying the term or any part of it. Each player has up to five minutes to solve ten terms. Passed cards return to the back of that player’s queue. Screenshot or download the final results and submit the image individually to the Canvas Lecture Participation assignment. Participation credit is based on completion.

## Phone verification

On iPhone Safari or Android Chrome, open the published game directly. Turn the phone to the orientation you intend to play in. Tap Enable motion, allow access if prompted, then hold the phone upright against your forehead with the display facing away and tap Calibrate. Hold still until ready. Start the turn. Tip the display down for Correct or up for Pass, then return to neutral. Hold a tilt to check that it registers once. Test portrait and both landscape directions, recalibrating when you rotate. Check the handoff, touch fallback, and wake lock. If there is no usable sensor or permission is declined, the visible Correct and Pass buttons remain available.

## Vocabulary

The 64-term bank in plant-propagation-heads-up/vocabulary.js is provisional because the exact Week 14 deck was unavailable. Review the terms against your slides before classroom use. Keep six categories with at least eight distinct terms in each. Every player draws from all six categories, with two terms from four randomly allocated categories and one from the remaining two. Terms are shuffled and shared across neither players nor retries as extra points.

## Privacy

The app uses in-memory state and client-side image generation. Refreshing the page clears the session. Student names and scores are never transmitted by the game.
